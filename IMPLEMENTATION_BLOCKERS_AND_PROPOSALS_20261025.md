## Evidence and execution interface

New episodes contain `attempts.jsonl` for HTTP request/response/outcome events, `actor_outcomes.jsonl` for independently persisted actor results, and either a completion seal or `failure.json`. The log root's append-only `attempt_census.jsonl` records prospective run creation/completion/failure. Its coverage explicitly excludes unrecorded historical attempts; it cannot repair a missing historical prefix.

No request headers or credentials are logged. Response bytes are retained exactly unless a credential must be redacted; those records explicitly retain the original digest and a redaction marker. Unknown costs, timeouts and interrupted attempts never become zero usage. A completed episode binds its attempt/outcome journals and reconstructs successful replies from raw provider responses. A failed campaign keeps links to completed siblings and its failed episode rather than writing a completion marker.

Live CLI invocations now additionally require `--max-http-attempts REQUEST_LIMIT --deadline-seconds SECONDS`. These are caller-declared operational bounds, not new default scientific sampling counts or a dollar cap. The request counter is shared across actors, runs, branches and retries in that invocation; separate terminals have separate counters. `--resolved-model EXACT_SERVER_ID` declares an alias's expected served version; without it, the requested model string is the expected identity. A different served version stops rather than silently changing populations.

Use `python3 -m collusion.preflight_budget --attempt-log /path/to/new/attempts.jsonl` for all-attempt accounting. The Haiku 5.5 cost estimate applies only to observed usage for that served model without caching; all other rates/unknown usage remain unpriced. The old `--usage-log` is explicitly an accepted-reply sample and cannot serve as a billable-call ledger. The reserve calculation now distinguishes a spend-capacity gap from the additional starting credits required while preserving the reserve.

The primary campaign analysis entry point is `collusion.amendment_analysis`, not the older descriptive `collusion.inference`. Reports preserve all six A3 fixed-rho values. Failure of the new conservative interval certificate means “not established,” not evidence that a sign reversal occurred.

PR #5 remains the separate correction that authenticates raw pretest rounds and recomputes the monopoly qualification and summary. This PR additionally validates the pretest's call evidence/configuration and must be reviewed in composition with #5. It overlaps PR #4's protocol-binding integration, with stricter result-row syntax; the PRs should not be merged blindly or treated as independent approvals of A10.

## Methodology proposals — NOT ACCEPTED OR EXECUTED

The following require a recorded scientific decision and any corresponding prospective amendment. This PR provides no override that silently admits them.

1. **Historical protocol/configuration transitions and output-cap cohorts.** Obtain the original successful, failed and restarted pretest/base evidence from the teammate. Decide whether/how each 1200-cap, 4096-cap and retry-policy cohort can contribute. Do not assume a successful low-cap trajectory would be identical under a different cap. Do not backfill unknown configuration as if it had been recorded, erase failed attempts, or replay successful runs to manufacture a homogeneous cohort. Current unknown/mismatched live configurations fail admission; any future provenance addendum must preserve original bytes and state its evidentiary basis.
2. **Structural rho assumptions and stronger identification.** The zero-excitation and out-of-domain guards fix demonstrated false qualification. They do not prove the common-intercept sticky-BR model, exclude heterogeneous targets, measure weak-identification power or establish structural inertia. Fixed effects, alternative estimators, additional excitation thresholds and calibration interventions remain proposals. A3 abstention remains the fallback.
3. **Parser/retry population policy.** The existing normalization and eight-attempt schema-retry policy remain visible in the effective configuration. Invalid string/field normalization is diagnosed and cannot qualify evidence downstream. Replacing this with a stricter immediate schema rejection, changing schema retry counts, reinterpreting historical normalized replies, or pooling across those policies requires an explicit decision. Complete-output and served-model checks enforce the stated response/model contract; their new collector version cannot silently admit historical configurations.
4. **Branch order and serving coupling.** The existing sham-before-cut order is unchanged. Balanced/interleaved scheduling would change the method and must be frozen before new fork data. Equal vLLM seeds are not a qualified online determinism guarantee; runtime/model revision pinning, batch invariance and whether sham2 uses a fresh or matched stream require separate qualification. No GPU was launched.
5. **Intervention validity and inference extensions.** Keep all prespecified fork points, including cuts that are rises relative to the simultaneous sham. A changed cut baseline, stability/exclusion rule, extra magnitudes, discounted or optimized multi-period deviations, additional estimators and multiple-testing/noise calibration are new design choices. The current 12-sham noise floor stays descriptive. The conservative all-rho bound implements the existing robustness requirement; narrowing it to a finite grid instead would require an amendment.
6. **Historical missing attempt coverage.** Newly durable journals cannot reconstruct earlier discarded replies, successful siblings or invoices. Inventory actual available history and mark the rest unavailable. Any analysis or budget decision depending on missing historical coverage remains qualified.
